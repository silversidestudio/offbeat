# OFFBEAT artwork provenance

Three original images were generated specifically for this fictional portfolio concept using the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool on 12 October 2026. They do not depict documented real venues, actual events or real customers. No third-party stock photographs or album covers were copied. Public captions and the concept disclosure identify the imagery as concept artwork.

Final web assets are in `assets/images/`: `listening-room-{640,1200}.webp`, `room-detail-{640,1200}.webp`, `record-stack-{640,1200}.webp`, and the social crop `offbeat-social.jpg`. Responsive WebP versions were resized and encoded from the original output; no aesthetic modifications were made. The original generated PNG files remain in the Codex generated-images directory.

## Fonts

Barlow Condensed, DM Sans and Libre Caslon Display are served locally from their Latin WOFF2 subsets. They were downloaded from Google Fonts. The SIL Open Font License files are included in `assets/fonts/`; retain them when distributing the font bundle.
