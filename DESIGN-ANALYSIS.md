# Reference analysis — La Revoltosa

Inspected https://larevoltosa.es/ and its Products page in the real browser at desktop and 390 × 844 mobile sizes, 11 October 2026.

- **Identity:** dominant vermilion and warm cream. The limited palette connects typography, interface, line illustration and product imagery.
- **Typography:** very tall condensed uppercase contrasts with broad, heavy slab serif words. Scale and contrast do most of the compositional work; small copy is secondary.
- **Composition:** generous full-screen moments, central headlines, floating bubbles and product bottles crossing text. Large illustrative scenes and irregular wave boundaries replace conventional cards.
- **Motion:** the opening initially fills with red, then type appears. Scrolling brings a large bottle across the headline and reveals an oversized pool illustration and video. Bubbles move independently. Scroll state feels choreographed rather than a simple sequence of blocks.
- **Navigation:** compact persistent menu opens a cream full-screen panel with giant stacked links. On mobile the menu sits near the bottom; headings simplify while retaining the contrasting typefaces.
- **Products:** a dramatic typographic introduction leads into product-focused, coloured scenes with oversized bottles, illustrations and conversational copy. During mobile scroll I observed overlapping transitional text, a useful caution against excessive scroll choreography.
- **Distinctiveness:** a specific cultural voice, a repeated visual motif with meaning, illustration at architectural scale, and transitions that make the page feel like a performance.
